# LOTO-001 Lockout/Tagout – Press Line P-12

Document: LOTO-001 | Revision: 4 | Date: 2025-09-01 | Owner: Health and Safety

## 1 When lockout/tagout is required

Lockout/tagout is required before any work inside the press, in the die area, on the hydraulic system or on electrical equipment of line P-12. This includes clearing jammed parts, cleaning the die and changing the hydraulic oil. Setup mode alone does not replace lockout/tagout.

## 2 Energy sources of the HP-250

| Energy | Isolation point | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| Electrical power | Main switch Q0 on the electrical cabinet | Try to start the pump from the HMI: no reaction |
| Hydraulic pressure | Accumulator dump valve V-3 | Pressure gauge P-3 shows 0 bar |
| Compressed air | Ball valve PV-1 with exhaust | Air gauge shows 0 bar |
| Gravity (slide) | Two safety blocks SB-P12 under the slide | Slide rests on the blocks |

## 3 Procedure

1. Notify the shift supervisor and the operators of the line that lockout will be applied.
2. Stop the press: move the slide to the lowest safe position in setup mode and stop the hydraulic pump.
3. Place both safety blocks under the slide and lower the slide onto them.
4. Switch off main switch Q0 and close the compressed air valve PV-1.
5. Open the accumulator dump valve V-3 and wait until gauge P-3 shows 0 bar.
6. Every person who works on the press attaches a personal red padlock and a tag with name, date and phone extension to each isolation point. For more than two persons, use the group lockout box.
7. Verify zero energy: try to start the pump from the HMI, check gauges P-3 and the air gauge.
8. Carry out the work.

## 4 Removing the lockout

1. Remove tools and materials from the press and close all guards.
2. Inform everyone that the press will be re-energized.
3. Each person removes only their own padlock and tag. A lock may never be removed by another person; in exceptional cases only the plant manager can authorize removal after confirming the owner is not on site.
4. Close valve V-3, open valve PV-1, switch on main switch Q0 and remove the safety blocks in setup mode.

## 5 Training

All operators, setters and maintenance technicians of line P-12 must complete lockout/tagout training once a year. Training records are kept by the Health and Safety department.
